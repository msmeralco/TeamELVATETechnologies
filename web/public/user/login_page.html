About Meralco

Powering progress and smart grid innovation across the Philippines

For over a century, the Manila Electric Company (Meralco) has been committed to delivering safe, reliable, and sustainable electricity to millions of homes and enterprises across Metro Manila and nearby provinces.

Through Meralco Go and our ongoing smart-grid modernization, we empower every Filipino household with cleaner energy choices, net-metering support, and seamless digital self-service.
